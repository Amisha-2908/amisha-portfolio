Featured project · Power BI + Power Automate

SOP Intelligence: are 30 stores following the rules, and what should each one fix?

A compliance command centre for a multi-city store chain. It scores every store and every employee on hygiene, operations and customer service, then turns each gap into a clear next step.

Built in
Power BI Desktop, DAX, Power Automate
Covers
30 stores, 252 employees, 6 months of compliance records
Pages
Overview, analysis, what-if planner, store and employee deep-dives
Automation
One click emails each supervisor their team's compliance list

The whole chain on one screen

Leaders see in five seconds how the business is doing and where to look first.

  • 54% compliance across 30 stores, with the best store (63%) and the weakest (47%) named automatically.
  • The monthly trend shows compliance climbing to 62% in April, so the team can see whether changes are working.
  • Filter by city, SOP category, department, store or date, and every chart updates together.

Click a store, see its story, email the fix

Any store opens into its own page. The Send Email button sends the supervisor a list of their staff, each person's compliance rate and pending training.

  • Compares the store against the best and the weakest store in the chain.
  • Shows which department is slipping. Here, Front House and Kitchen are at about 50%, while Management is at 91%.
  • Adds the customer's view: 2,004 ratings averaging 3.11 out of 5.

Set a target and get the plan to reach it

A manager types the goal, and the dashboard works out exactly how much each area has to improve.

  • Enter a target of 60% and the store's current 51% shows a 9-point gap, which means 67 more compliant checks.
  • Choose how much hygiene, operations and customer service should each contribute. The required rate for each area updates immediately.
  • A ready list of suggested actions sits alongside, such as "Complete safety checks daily".

A fair league table you can tune

Stores are ranked on a combined score, and the business decides what matters most.

  • Set the weight of each area, for example 50% customer service, 20% hygiene and 30% operations, and the ranking re-orders.
  • Show the top or bottom 10 stores, or any other number, with one input.
  • The spread chart shows 29 of 30 stores sitting between 50% and 75%, so the team knows how much headroom there is.

Every person gets their own page

Opens with a personal greeting, then shows how this person is doing and where they need support.

  • Personal compliance (94%) next to the store's (53%), so strong performers stand out.
  • The most frequent incident is named in plain words: "Unprepared for Opening".
  • Training status by trainer, and a monthly trend that flags a slide from 100% to 82%.

From numbers to coaching

Supervisors see who needs which training, next to practical tips they can use in the next shift.

  • Search any employee by name and see their department, role, compliance and training.
  • Incomplete training stands out so supervisors know who to schedule next.
  • An "Areas of improvement" list gives concrete actions, such as "Prepare checklist the night before".

Hover to understand

Point at any chart and a tooltip explains each rule in plain words, for example "Store must open at 10:00 and close at 22:00", with its compliance rate.

Customer voice at a glance

Customer feedback comments turn into a word cloud, so the most repeated words, such as "inconsistent" or "outstanding", are easy to spot.
